 Recall the definitions of $\alpha$-strong convexity and $\beta$-smoothness: for all $x,y$
 \[ \frac{\alpha}{2} \|y - x\|_{2}^{2} \leq f(y) - f(x) - \langle \nabla f(x), y - x \rangle \leq \frac{\beta}{2} \|y - x\|_{2}^{2} .   \]
 We will examine equivalent $1$-st and $2$-nd order definitions of these properties. For this question, assume $f$ is twice continuously differentiable. 
 \begin{enumerate}
    \item Show if $f : \R^{n} \to \R$ is $\alpha$-strongly convex or $\beta$-smooth, then the restriction $g(t) := f(x + t v)$ is $\alpha \|v\|_{2}^{2}$-strongly convex and $\beta \|v\|_{2}^{2}$-smooth for any $x, v \in \R^{n}$;
     \item Show $\alpha$-strong convexity and $\beta$-smoothness are equivalent to the conditions $\alpha I \preceq \nabla^{2} f(x)$ and $\nabla^{2} f(x) \preceq \beta I$, respectively.
